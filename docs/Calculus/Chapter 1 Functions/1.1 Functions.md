# Functions

A function assigns exactly one output to every input in its domain.

## Function Notation

Functions are written as

$$
f(x)
$$

Evaluate by substituting the input.

### Example

Given

$$
f(x)=3x+2
$$

$$
f(4)=14
$$

## Domain

The domain is the set of all allowable inputs.

Common restrictions:

### Division by Zero

Denominators cannot equal zero.

Example

$$
f(x)=\frac1{x-3}
$$

Domain:

$$
x\neq3
$$

### Even Roots

The expression inside an even root must satisfy

$$
\text{inside}\ge0
$$

Example

$$
\sqrt{x-5}
$$

Domain

$$
x\ge5
$$

### Logarithms

Arguments must be positive.

$$
\ln(x+2)
$$

Domain

$$
x>-2
$$

## Piecewise Functions

Evaluate using the equation corresponding to the interval containing the input.

### Example

$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
x^2,&x<0\\
2x+1,&x\ge0
\end{cases}
$$

$$
f(-2)=4
$$

$$
f(3)=7
$$

### Exam Tips

- Domain questions almost always involve fractions, radicals, or logarithms.
- Always determine the interval before evaluating a piecewise function.
- Use parentheses when substituting negative numbers.
