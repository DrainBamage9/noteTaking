# Mechanics
Mechanics is the study of forces on bodies
There are a handful of branches
- Fluid Mechanics
- Deformable Body Mechanics
- Rigid Body Mechanics
  - Statics: $a = 0$
  - Dynamics: $a\cancel{=}0$

Specifically statics is a form of dynamics where the system, or body, is assumed to be in equilibirum. Meaning all the forces are balanced and no motion occurs. Hence acceleration = 0.
