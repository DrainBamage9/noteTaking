# Moment of a Couple
A couple consists of two equal, parallel, and opposite forces taht are seperated only by perpendicular distance. A couple produces pure rotational with no net force.
$$
\boxed{\sum\mathbf{F}=0} \\\\
\text{but} \\\\
\boxed{\sum\mathbf{M}\neq{}0}
$$

The magnitude of a couple moment is:
$$
\boxed{M=Fd}
$$
Where:
- $F$ is the magnitude of a force.
- $d$ is the perpendicular distance between the forces' lines of action.
The direction is determined by the right-hand rule. 

## Vector Formulation
The moment of a couple can be found by using either force:
$$
\boxed{\mathbf{M}=\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{F}
$$
where $\mathbf{r}$ extends from any point on one force's line of action to the corresponding point on the other force's line of action.
