# Moments about a Specified Axis
Sometimes mements produced by a force around a specific axis must be determined.

## Scalar Analysis
The moment about a specified axis is by:
$$
M_a = Fd_a
$$

## Vector Analysis
To find the moment of a force $\mathbf{F}$ about the $y$ axis using vector analysis. We find the moment about any point $O$ on the y axis using the typical moment equation. $\mathbf{M_o}=\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{F}$. The compoinent $M_y$ along the y axis is the projection of $\mathbf{M_O}$ onto the y-axis. This can be found by using the dot product. $M_y=\mathbf{u_y}\cdot\mathbf{M_O}=\mathbf{u_y}\cdot(\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{F})$ where $\mathbf{u_y}$ is the unit vector for the y-axis.
$$
M_y=\mathbf{u_y}\cdot(\mathbf{r}\times\mathbf{F})
$$
Once we find the moment about the y-axis ($M_y$), we can go about find its vector components $\mathbf{M_y}$.
$$
\mathbf{M_y}=M_y\mathbf{u_y}
$$

## Example
A force is applied at point $A$ relative to the point $O$.
$$
\mathbf{r_{OA}}=[3,2,4]m
$$
The applied force is:
$$
\mathbf{F}=[50,-30,40]N
$$
The axis $AB$ passes through the points:
$$
A(1,2,1),\quad{}B(4,6,3)
$$
Find the moment $\mathbf{F}$ about the axis $AB$.

Step 1: Find the moment about $O$
$$
\mathbf{M_O}=\mathbf{r_{OA}}\times\mathbf{F}
$$
$$
\mathbf{M_O}=
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf{i}, & \mathbf{j}, & \mathbf{k} \\\\
3, & 2, & 4 \\\\
50, & -30, & 40
\end{bmatrix}
$$
$$
\mathbf{M_O}=[200,80,-190]N\cdot{}m
$$

Step 2: Find the Unit Vector along Axis $AB$

Find the position vector from $A$ to $B$:
$$
\mathbf{r_{AB}}=[(4-1),(6-2),(3-1)]\\\\
\mathbf{r_{AB}}=[3,4,2]
$$
Its magnitude is:
$$
\mathbf{r_{AB}}=\sqrt{3^2+4^2+2^2}=\sqrt{29}
$$
Therefor we know the unit vector is:
$$
\mathbf{u_{AB}}=\frac{[3,4,2]}{\sqrt{29}}
$$

Step 3: Project the moment onto the axis $AB$

The moment about the axis is:
$$
\mathbf{M_{AB}}=\mathbf{u_{AB}}\cdot\mathbf{M_O}
$$
Then substitue in our variables:
$$
\mathbf{M_{AB}}=\frac{[3,4,2]}{\sqrt{29}}\cdot[200,80,-190] \\\\
\mathbf{M_{AB}}=\frac{600+320-380}{\sqrt{29}} \\\\
\boxed{\mathbf{M_{AB}}=\frac{540}{\sqrt{29}}\approx{}100.3N\cdot{}m}
$$
