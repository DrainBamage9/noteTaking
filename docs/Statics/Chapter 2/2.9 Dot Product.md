# Dot Product

## Definition

The **dot product** determines the scalar relationship between two vectors.
What does this actually mean? Given two arbitrary vectors $\vec{\mathbf{A}}$ and $\vec{\mathbf{B}}$, it is how much of A is felt along B. 

For vectors $\mathbf{A}$ and $\mathbf{B}$:

$$
\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B}=AB\cos\theta
$$

where $\theta$ is the angle between the vectors.

## Laws of Operation

1. Commutative Law : $\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}}=\vec{\mathbf{B}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{A}}$
2. Scalar Multiplication: $a(\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}})=(a\vec{\mathbf{A}})\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}}=\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot(a \vec{\mathbf{B}})$
3. Distributive Law: $\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot(\vec{\mathbf{B}}+\vec{\mathbf{D}}) = (\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}})+(\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{D}})$

## Cartesian Form

For:

$$
\vec{\mathbf{A}}=A_x\mathbf{i}+A_y\mathbf{j}+A_z\mathbf{k}
$$

$$
\vec{\mathbf{B}}=B_x\mathbf{i}+B_y\mathbf{j}+B_z\mathbf{k}
$$

the dot product is:

$$
\boxed{
\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}}
=
A_xB_x+A_yB_y+A_zB_z
}
$$

The result is a **scalar**, not a vector.

## Angle Between Vectors

The angle between two vectors can be found from:

$$
\boxed{
\theta=
\cos^{-1}
\left(
\frac{\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}}}{AB}
\right)
}
$$

## Orthogonal Vectors

Two vectors are **perpendicular** when their dot product is zero:

$$
\boxed{\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{B}}=0}
$$

since:

$$
\cos90^\circ=0
$$

## Projection

The **scalar projection** of $\vec{\mathbf{A}}$ onto $\vec{\mathbf{B}}$ is:

$$
\boxed{
A_B=A\cos\theta
}
$$

Using the dot product:

$$
\boxed{
A_B=\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{u}}_B
}
$$

where $\vec{\mathbf{u}}_B$ is the unit vector in the direction of $\vec{\mathbf{B}}$.

The corresponding **vector projection** is:

$$
\boxed{
\vec{\mathbf{A}}_B=(\vec{\mathbf{A}}\cdot\vec{\mathbf{u}}_B)\vec{\mathbf{u}}_B
}
$$

## Procedure

1. Express both vectors in Cartesian form.
2. Multiply corresponding components.
3. Add the products.
4. Use the result to determine the angle or projection, if required.
