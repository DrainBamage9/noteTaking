# Position Vectors

## Position Vector

A **position vector** defines the location of one point relative to another point.

For a point $A$ with coordinates $(x_A,y_A,z_A)$:

$$
\mathbf{\vec r}_A=x_A\mathbf{i}+y_A\mathbf{j}+z_A\mathbf{k}
$$

## Position Vector Between Two Points

The position vector from point $A$ to point $B$ is:

$$
\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}=\mathbf{\vec r}_B-\mathbf{\vec r}_A
$$

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}=(x_B-x_A)\mathbf{i}+(y_B-y_A)\mathbf{j}+(z_B-z_A)\mathbf{k}}
$$

The vector points **from $A$ to $B$**.

### Distance Between Points

The magnitude of $\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}$ gives the distance between the two points:

$$
r_{AB}=\sqrt{(x_B-x_A)^2+(y_B-y_A)^2+(z_B-z_A)^2}
$$

## Unit Vector

A unit vector defines the **direction** of a vector while having a magnitude of 1.

$$
\mathbf{\vec u}_{AB}=\frac{\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}}{r_{AB}}
$$

## Example: Position Vector

Given:

$$
A(2,-1,3), \qquad B(6,4,-2)
$$

Find the position vector $\mathbf{r}_{AB}$ from $A$ to $B$.

### $\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j},\mathbf{k}$ Method

$$
\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}=\mathbf{\vec r}_B-\mathbf{\vec r}_A
$$

$$
\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}
=(6-2)\mathbf{i}+(4-(-1))\mathbf{j}+(-2-3)\mathbf{k}
$$

$$
\boxed{\mathbf{r}_{AB}=4\mathbf{i}+5\mathbf{j}-5\mathbf{k}}
$$

### Matrix Method

Represent the coordinates as matrix, this is also called a row vector:

$$
\mathbf{\vec r}_A=
\begin{bmatrix}
2, & -1, & 3
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf{\vec r}_B=
\begin{bmatrix}
6, & 4, & -2
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Subtract:

$$
\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}
=
\mathbf{\vec r}_B-\mathbf{\vec r}_A
$$

$$
=
\begin{bmatrix}
6, & 4, & -2
\end{bmatrix}
-
\begin{bmatrix}
2, & -1, & 3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}=
\begin{bmatrix}
4, & 5, & -5
\end{bmatrix}}
$$

Both representations describe the **same vector**:

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf{\vec r}_{AB}
=
4\mathbf{i}+5\mathbf{j}-5\mathbf{k}
\equiv
\begin{bmatrix}
4, & 5, & -5
\end{bmatrix}}
$$
