# Addition of Cartesian Vectors

## Vector Addition

Cartesian vectors are added by summing their corresponding **x, y, and z components**.

$$
\mathbf{\vec R}=\mathbf{\vec A}+\mathbf{\vec B}
$$

$$
\mathbf{\vec R}=(A_x+B_x)\mathbf{i}+(A_y+B_y)\mathbf{j}+(A_z+B_z)\mathbf{k}
$$

### Component Form

For multiple vectors:

$$
\mathbf{\vec R}=\sum\mathbf{\vec F}
$$

$$
R_x=\sum F_x,\qquad R_y=\sum F_y,\qquad R_z=\sum F_z
$$

Therefore,

$$
\mathbf{\vec R}=R_x\mathbf{i}+R_y\mathbf{j}+R_z\mathbf{k}
$$

or in my prefered form, matrix format,
$$
\boxed{\mathbf{\vec R}=[R_x, R_y, R_z]}
$$
