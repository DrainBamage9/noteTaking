# Equilibrium of a Particle

## Definition of Equilibrium

A particle is in equilibrium when it remains at rest or moves with constant velocity. For a particle in static equilibrium,

$$
\sum \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{0}
$$

This is the fundamental condition for equilibrium of a particle.

In words:
- The vector sum of all forces acting on the particle is zero.
- There is no net force; therefore, there is no acceleration $\mathbf{a} = 0$.

According to Newton's First Law, if the resultant force is zero, then the particle is either at rest or in uniform motion.
