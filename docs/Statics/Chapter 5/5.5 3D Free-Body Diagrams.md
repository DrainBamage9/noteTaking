# 3D Free-Body Diagram
Similar to 2D FBDs, there exists Free-Body Diagrams for three dimensions. Rigid bodies in three-dimensions must satisfy six independent equations, often refered to by 6-DOF. (6 degrees of freedom, 3 axial and 3 rotational).
## Force Equations
$$
\sum F_x=0,\qquad \sum F_y=0,\qquad \sum F_z=0
$$

## Moment Equations
$$
\sum M_x=0,\qquad \sum M_y=0,\qquad \sum M_z=0
$$

Moments are calculated about the coordinate axes or a selected point using:

$$
\mathbf M_O=\mathbf r\times\mathbf F
$$

## Support Reactions

| Connection / Support | Unknown Reactions |
|---|---|
| **Cable / Rope** | One unknown force along the cable |
| **Smooth Surface Support** | One unknown force which acts perpendicular to the surface |
| **Roller** | One unknown force which acts perpendicular to the surface |
| **Ball & Socket** | Three unknowns which are the rectangular/axial force components. (x,y,z) |
| **Single Journal Bearing** | Four unknowns. Two axial force components along the x and z axis, and the moments about those axises.|
| **Journal Bearing with square shaft**| Five unknowns, the same as from the single journal bearing plus the moments along the square shaft|
| **Single Thrust Bearing** | The same as single journal bearing plus an axial force along the shaft. |
| **Single Smooth Pin** | Five unknowns. The three rectangular/axial forces and the moments about the z and y axis.|
| **Single hinge** | Five Unknowns. Three axial forces plus the moments about the x and z axis. |
| **Fixed Support** | Six Unknowns. Three axial foces and moments about those axises|
