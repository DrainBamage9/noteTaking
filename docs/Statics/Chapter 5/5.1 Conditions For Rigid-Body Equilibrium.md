# Conditions for Ridid-Body Equilibrium.
A body subjected to external forces and couple moments systems muyst have a net force and moment of zero. Undergoing no linear or angular acceleration. Note this does not mean the rigid body is not moving, only its undergoing no net acceleration.
## Equation of Equilibrium
For a two-dimensional rigid body:

- $F_R=\sum F=0$ 
- $(M_R)_O=\sum M_O =0$
