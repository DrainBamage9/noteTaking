# Free-Body Diagrams (FBD)

Isolate the body and replace each support or connection with its associated reaction forces and couple moments.

| Connection / Support | Unknown Reactions |
|---|---|
| **Cable / Rope** | One tension force along the cable |
| **Weigthless Link** | One force along the link |
| **Roller / Rocker** | One force perpendicular to the supporting surface |
| **Smooth Surface** | One normal force perpendicular to the surface |
| **Pin in Smooth Slot** | One force perpendicular to the slot |
| **Pin Connected to collar on rod**| One normal force perpendicular to the rod|
| **Pin / Hinge** | Two force components: $F_x,\ F_y$ |
| **Collar on Smooth Rod** | Couple moment and force perpendicular to the rod|
| **Fixed Support** | Two force components and one couple moment: $F_x,\ F_y,\ M$ |

### Important Notes

- Always solve equilibrium problems by first drawing the Free Body Diagram.
- In **2D**, a rigid body has at most three independent equilibrium equations: \(\sum F_x=0\), \(\sum F_y=0\), and \(\sum M_O=0\).
- Reaction forces act in directions constrained by the connection. If the direction is unknown, assume one and interpret a negative answer as the opposite direction.
- **Smooth** means frictionless; **rough** means friction may act.
- A **two-force member** has forces at its two ends that are equal, opposite, and collinear.
- Include only external reactions on the FBD of the isolated body. Internal forces between parts of the same isolated system cancel out.
