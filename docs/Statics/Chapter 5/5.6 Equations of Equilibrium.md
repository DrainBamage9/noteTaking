# Equations of Equilibrium
The three-dimensional equilibrium equations are approximatly the same as two-dimensional. That being the sum of all forces and all moments must equal zero.
$$
\boxed{
\sum F=0 \quad
\sum M_O=0}
$$
Therefor:

$$
\sum F_x=0 \\\\
\sum F_y=0 \\\\
\sum F_z=0 \\\\
\text{and}\\\\
\sum M_x=0 \\\\
\sum M_y=0 \\\\
\sum M_z=0
$$
